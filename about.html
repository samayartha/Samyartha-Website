ABOUT

Diagnose it, redesign it, stay through delivery.

Samayartha Advisory helps mid-market Indian manufacturing, industrial, and B2B technology companies diagnose what's constraining growth or margin, redesign the operating model around it, and stay through execution — not just the slide deck.

The name comes from samaya (time) and artha (purpose/wealth) — the belief that the right structure, applied at the right time, is what turns effort into outcomes.
